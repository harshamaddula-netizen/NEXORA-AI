01 — Foundation

About Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is the science of building machines that perceive, reason, learn and act. From recommending your next song to diagnosing disease, AI quietly powers the modern world.

01

What is AI?

AI is a branch of computer science dedicated to creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence — understanding language, recognising images, solving problems and making decisions.

02

How it Learns

Instead of being programmed line by line, modern AI systems are trained. They study enormous datasets, find hidden patterns and steadily improve their accuracy through a process called machine learning.

03

Why it Matters

AI multiplies human capability. It accelerates scientific discovery, automates repetitive work, personalises education and helps organisations make faster, smarter, data-driven decisions.

02 — Core Technologies

The Engines Behind Modern AI

Four powerful disciplines work together to give machines their intelligence.

Machine Learning

Algorithms that learn from data instead of explicit instructions. ML powers fraud detection, recommendation engines and demand forecasting across every industry.

  • Regression
  • Clustering
  • Random Forests

Deep Learning

Multi-layered neural networks loosely inspired by the human brain. Deep learning is the breakthrough behind ChatGPT, self-driving cars and hyper-realistic image generation.

  • CNNs
  • Transformers
  • GANs
“ai”

Natural Language Processing

The ability of machines to read, understand and generate human language. NLP drives chatbots, real-time translation, summarisation and voice assistants.

  • Chatbots
  • Translation
  • Sentiment

Computer Vision

Teaching machines to see and interpret the visual world — from medical scans and factory quality checks to facial recognition and autonomous navigation.

  • Detection
  • Segmentation
  • OCR

04 — The Toolbox

Popular AI Tools

These widely-used platforms show how AI is already at work for millions of people every day.

ChatGPT

Conversational AI

OpenAI's language assistant that drafts, explains, codes and brainstorms in natural language — the fastest-adopted product in history.

Midjourney

Image Generation

Turns written descriptions into striking, studio-quality artwork, transforming design, advertising and concept art workflows.

TensorFlow

ML Framework

Google's open-source library for building and training machine learning models, used from research labs to production apps.

PyTorch

Deep Learning

A flexible, research-friendly framework powering much of today's cutting-edge deep learning, from transformers to diffusion models.

Copilot

An AI pair-programmer that autocompletes entire functions, explains unfamiliar code and dramatically speeds up software development.

Gemini

Multimodal AI

Google's multimodal model that understands text, images, audio and code together — a single assistant for every kind of information.
